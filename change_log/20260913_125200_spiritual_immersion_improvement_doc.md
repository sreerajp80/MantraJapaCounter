## 1. Overview of Changes

Created a comprehensive, spiritually grounded improvements and future roadmap document (`docs/improvements.md`) for the **Mantra Japa Counter** application.

The document directly reflects the ethos of Sanatana Dharma:
- Japa is a practice of *Bhavana* (deep immersion, loving contemplation, and presence), not a race or mechanical quota.
- Rejection of toxic gamification (streak anxiety, competitive leaderboards, rush clicking).
- Retaining 100% offline privacy, zero telemetry, and zero cloud tracking.

---

## 2. Detailed Modifications

1. **`docs/improvements.md`** [NEW]:
   - **Spiritual Philosophy & Core Principles**: Explains Patanjali's *"Tajjapas tadartha-bhavanam"*, the sacred role of the count as an anchor (*alambana*), and compassionate sadhana continuity.
   - **Improvements to Existing Features**:
     - *Blind / Full-Screen Tap Mode* for eyes-closed chanting (*Antarmukha*).
     - *Hardware Volume Button Counting* for discreet practice in a pocket or bag.
     - *Tactile Bead-Rolling Drag Gesture* simulating rolling physical beads with the thumb.
     - *Meru Bead Sacred Pause* at bead 108 for mindful breath and reverence.
     - *Mindful Pacing & Anti-Rushing Awareness* to gently discourage hurried tapping.
     - *Authentic Sacred Acoustic Soundscapes* (bronze temple bell, Tibetan singing bowl, shankha, organic wood bead clicks) replacing synthetic DTMF tones.
     - *Sadhana Flow vs. Streaks* replacing streak anxiety with warm, guilt-free spiritual remembrance.
     - *Time in Stillness* tracking contemplative minutes alongside counts.
     - *Selective Counter QR Sync* and optional local export encryption.
   - **Unique New Features**:
     - *Akhanda / Dhyana Mode (Numberless Immersion)*: Screen displays only a gently breathing Diya flame or lotus mandala while the app quietly tracks the count in the background.
     - *Artha Bhavana (Mantra Meaning, Deity & Dhyana Shloka)*: Stores script text, word-by-word meaning, and pre-sitting reflection verses.
     - *Offline Tanpura / Sacred Drone*: Continuous acoustic keynote (*Sa-Pa*) in various pitches for sonic grounding.
     - *Post-Session Stillness & Sadhana Diary*: 1-minute silent reflection period and private offline notes.
     - *Sacred Sankalpa (Vow) Ceremony*: Reverent guided setup for long-term spiritual resolves.
     - *100% Offline Panchanga & Auspicious Timings Companion*: Algorithmically calculates Brahma Muhurta and Sandhya times offline without GPS/network calls.
     - *Visual Bead Material Customization*: Rudraksha, Tulsi, Sandalwood, Lotus Seed, and Sphatika crystal styling.
     - *Universal Sacred Traditions Mode*: Presets for 108 Japa Mala, 33/99 Tasbih, and 59-bead Rosary.
   - **Roadmap & Safeguards**: 4-phase rollout matrix and architectural safeguards for battery life, offline privacy, and SQLite schema compatibility.
