The new formatter does these kinds of changes:

- Adds a blank line between `dart:` imports and `package:` imports.
- Keeps a long test name on the same line as `test(` and puts the body one level less
  deep, so the trailing comma after the closure goes away.
- Changes other line breaks and indentation.
