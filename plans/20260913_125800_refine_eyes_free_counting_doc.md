# Implementation Plan — Refine Eyes-Free Counting in Improvements Document

**Status:** completed

## Overview

Refine `docs/improvements.md` based on user feedback regarding eyes-closed chanting.

### Context & Clarification
The app already excels at eyes-free counting:
- The large central medallion occupies almost the entire screen width, making it effortless to tap without looking.
- Reaching bead 108 is already clearly signaled through both sound (mala beep/tone) and distinct haptic vibration.
- Sadhakas can already close their eyes, immerse in their japa, and know when a mala is completed without ever looking at the screen.

The improvement document currently mischaracterizes this by suggesting users *must* look at the screen to count. We will correct this to celebrate the app's existing eyes-free design and frame future ideas (like full-viewport edge tapping, volume keys, and pocket mode) as ergonomic conveniences rather than a missing core capability.
